Community layout

Kismet Crest is laid out along a private road that enters from Hawkins Lane and winds east to a loop at the far end. Lots 1 to 16 sit near the entrance, lots 17 to 26 line the middle of the road, and lots 27 to 38 surround the eastern loop. Everything outside the individual lots, including the private road, landscaping, and carport areas, is Lot A, the common area owned by the association.

Dues and payments

Annual dues
$200 per lot, as last recorded in 1996
Due date
July 1, in one payment. It must be received by July 31.
Late payments
Unpaid dues accrue 9% annual interest after 30 days and become a lien on the lot.
Increases
The board may raise dues up to 3% a year. Anything more needs a two-thirds vote of members.

Dues maintain the common area and pay its property taxes. Owners of the carports assigned to lots 1–16, 22, 23, 31, and 32 maintain those carports themselves.

Key rules from the CC&Rs

Exterior changes
Get written approval from the Architectural Committee before building, fencing, landscaping, or changing a home’s exterior. No answer within 30 days counts as approval.
Common area
No changes to its landscaping or trimming of its trees and shrubs without written approval.
Parking
Boats, trailers, RVs, and trucks must be kept inside an enclosed garage. Don’t block garages, driveways, or walks.
Pets
Household pets only, not for commercial breeding.
Signs
One for-sale or for-rent sign of up to five square feet.
Trash
Keep it in clean, closed containers.
Shared walls
Neighbors split the cost of repairing shared walls and their exterior roofs, gutters, and siding.
Insurance
Insure each home for its full replacement value.
